1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SilentSurge: Smart Planner + (“SilentSurge: Smart Planner +,” the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Android or iOS App, contact support, use optional AI or voice features, view advertisements, enable analytics or crash diagnostics, or make a purchase.

SilentSurge: Smart Planner + is a local-first productivity and planning application. Most planner content remains on the device. Network communication is used only for features that require it, including advertising, purchases, optional analytics and crash diagnostics, support communications, and optional AI features. Certain AI requests are securely relayed through Google Firebase / Google Cloud infrastructure to the AI provider; this does not create SilentSurge cloud sync or a SilentSurge account.

Where consent or another specific choice is required, the App provides an appropriate in-app, consent-management, marketplace, or operating-system control.

2. Information stored on your device

You may create or enter tasks, subtasks, goals, schedules, reminders, completion records, notes, handwriting, planner layouts, theme and music preferences, notification settings, and AI output that you choose to save or apply.

During onboarding, SilentSurge: Smart Planner + offers a display-name field. You may use a nickname, initials, alias, pseudonym, or other preferred display name; a legal name is not required. The display name and other planner content are stored locally on the device.

SilentSurge: Smart Planner + does not require a SilentSurge: Smart Planner + account or login and does not currently provide its own cloud sync, account-based backup, or cross-device recovery. Local content may be lost if you uninstall the App, clear its storage, reset or lose the device, or experience device or storage failure. Device operating systems, device backups, device-management software, or other applications may independently affect how local data is copied, restored, or deleted.

Planner content is not transmitted merely because it exists in the App. It leaves the device only when you deliberately use a feature that requires transmission, such as submitting an AI prompt, sending a support message, or completing a marketplace purchase.

3. AI features

SilentSurge: Smart Planner + provides optional AI-powered features using DeepSeek, operated by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. Availability, supported functions, and request limits may vary by subscription tier. Depending on the tier and feature, AI functionality may include planning and productivity assistance, general-purpose user questions, and AI-generated productivity insights.

AI processing occurs only when you deliberately use an AI feature that requires an online request. The App may transmit:

  • the text or other content that you intentionally submit through AI Coach or another user-initiated AI input;
  • language, formatting, safety, request-type, tier, date, and technical information needed to process, secure, validate, and return that request;
  • limited active-session context created within the AI feature when needed to produce a coherent response; and
  • for a user-initiated Premium AI Insight, limited derived planner information such as aggregate task, habit, or goal statistics, completion trends, covered or recent topic identifiers, and approved productivity-tip identifiers or metadata needed to generate the requested insight.

For Premium AI Insights, SilentSurge: Smart Planner + is designed to minimise the information sent. Raw task titles, notes, handwriting, the onboarding display name, and the complete planner are not sent merely to generate an insight. Ordinary planner content is not transmitted merely because it exists in the App.

For AI Coach, SilentSurge: Smart Planner + does not automatically add unrelated planner entries, schedules, goals, notes, handwriting, or the onboarding display name to your request. If you deliberately type, paste, dictate, or otherwise submit such information through AI Coach, it becomes part of the request.

AI requests are sent from the App to a Firebase Cloud Function operated for SilentSurge: Smart Planner + and then relayed to DeepSeek. The DeepSeek API credential is stored server-side using Google Cloud Secret Manager and is not intentionally embedded in the distributed App. Firebase App Check is used to help verify legitimate App requests and protect the AI relay from abuse. These infrastructure services may process technical request, network, security, attestation, and service-operation information necessary to provide and protect the feature.

Before the first AI request is transmitted, the App will identify DeepSeek as the AI provider, explain the categories of information sent and the purpose of the transmission, provide access to relevant provider information, and obtain any permission required by applicable law or platform policy. DeepSeek may process inputs, outputs, request logs, security signals, network information, and other technical metadata in connection with providing, securing, maintaining, and improving its services, subject to the applicable DeepSeek Open Platform terms and provider materials.

DeepSeek is based in China. Information transmitted for AI processing may be processed in China and in other locations used by DeepSeek or its service providers, subject to applicable law and safeguards described in this Policy.

SilentSurge: Smart Planner + does not maintain a permanent, user-visible server-side AI chat archive. The SilentSurge Firebase relay is not designed to store prompts, responses, planner data, or conversation history as an AI archive. If you save or apply an AI response, that response becomes ordinary device-local planner content. Closing the AI screen does not necessarily delete information already processed by DeepSeek.

Do not submit passwords, complete payment-card details, government identification numbers, confidential patient information, medical records, or other sensitive or confidential information that you do not want processed by an AI provider.

4. Voice Planner

Voice Planner is separate from the AI Coach. Microphone access begins only after you use the voice control and grant operating-system permission.

Voice Planner is designed to use on-device speech recognition. SilentSurge: Smart Planner + does not intentionally upload or retain raw microphone audio. A transcript remains local unless you deliberately submit that text through an online feature, such as an AI request or support message.

You may deny or revoke microphone access through your device settings. If a future version uses an external speech-recognition service, the App will provide an updated disclosure before that processing begins and this Policy will be updated where required.

6. Optional Firebase Analytics

SilentSurge: Smart Planner + uses Google Firebase Analytics only after you affirmatively enable Analytics through the App’s privacy controls. This choice is offered globally, not only in regions where consent is legally required. Analytics remains disabled unless you enable it, and you may disable future Analytics collection in Settings.

Depending on configuration and device settings, Firebase Analytics may process:

  • App interactions and feature-use events;
  • session and engagement information;
  • App version, device type, operating-system version, language, locale, and approximate region;
  • pseudonymous app-installation or device identifiers generated by the service; and
  • purchase or subscription events when configured for measurement.

We configure Analytics not to receive planner text, task titles, notes, handwriting, AI prompts, voice transcripts, support-message content, the onboarding display name, email addresses, or other user-entered free text. We do not deliberately assign a SilentSurge: Smart Planner + account identifier because the App has no SilentSurge: Smart Planner + account.

Analytics information is retained according to the Firebase retention settings selected for the App. Those settings are reviewed using the principles of data minimisation and storage limitation, taking into account the period reasonably necessary to understand feature use, evaluate product changes, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Aggregated or de-identified reporting may remain available for longer.

7. Optional Firebase Crashlytics

SilentSurge: Smart Planner + uses Google Firebase Crashlytics only after you affirmatively enable Crash Diagnostics through the App’s privacy controls. This choice is offered globally. Crash reporting remains disabled unless you enable it, and you may disable future Crashlytics collection in Settings.

Crashlytics may process technical information such as:

  • crash traces, errors, and diagnostic events;
  • App version and bundle or package information;
  • device model, operating-system version, processor, memory, and storage information;
  • Crashlytics and Firebase installation identifiers;
  • session identifiers and timestamps; and
  • technical circumstances surrounding a crash.

We configure Crashlytics not to receive planner content, AI prompts, voice transcripts, the onboarding display name, or other user-entered free text through custom logs, keys, breadcrumbs, exception messages, or user identifiers.

Google states that Firebase Crashlytics retains crash stack traces, extracted minidump data, and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.
